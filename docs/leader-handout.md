# YS Connection: a guide for leaders

This page tells you how to use the YS Connection app on your phone.

## 1. Open the app

Your admin gives you the web address of the app. Write it here:

`https://______________________`

Open the address in the browser on your phone.

## 2. Put the app on your Home Screen

Then the app opens like a normal phone app.

- **iPhone:** open the address in Safari. Select **Share**. Select **Add to Home Screen**. Select **Add**.
- **Android:** open the address in Chrome. Select the three dots at the top right. Select **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

Open the app from its icon on your Home Screen after that.

## 3. Sign in

Your admin gives you a username and a password. The leaders of your group share this login.

1. Type your username and your password.
2. Select **Sign In**.

If your password fails, ask your admin. Do not try many times.

## 4. "Who are you?"

The login is shared, so the app asks **Who are you?** before it sends something to Elvanto. Select your own name. Elvanto shows your name on what you send.

The phone remembers your name. Each leader uses their own phone.

## 5. Check-in

1. Select **More** > **Check-in**. On the service day, Home also shows a **Check-in** tile.
2. Type a name in the search box.
3. Select **Check in** next to the person.

## 6. Lifegroup report

1. Select **More** > **Lifegroup report**.
2. Find your group. Select **Report**.
3. Under **Who was there**, mark who came.
4. Select **Send**.

If you do not see **Lifegroup report**, your login does not send reports. Ask your admin.

## 7. "Waiting to send"

**Waiting to send** means the app has your change. The app sends it to Elvanto soon, usually in under a minute. You do not need to do it again.

If a line says to do it in Elvanto, tell your admin.

## 8. Change your password

Select **More** > **Change password**. The login is shared, so ask your admin first. Then tell the other leaders the new password.

## 9. Help

If your password fails, ask your admin.
