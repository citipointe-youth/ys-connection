What the mockup shows (spec §8.1)

  • Modelled on the camp app's daily check-in: service line, filters, a "still to come" count, the due list, then a collapsed "Checked in" list.
  • Name and grade only. No gender filter, no gender colour. Leaders show without a grade chip.
  • A tap is instant: the row moves at once and waits in the phone queue ("Waiting to send."; pill "2 waiting to send"). Status lines after that are the contract's leader lines. Your own row shows Undo.
  • Leaders are in the list, marked "Leader" instead of a grade.
  • The banner reports the console: practice mode per type, check-out not connected yet, or the console offline. "In Elvanto." = checked in on the kiosk (no app row).
  • New person opens the existing form; its "Check them in to this service" tick sends a first-timer check-in.
  • Reached from More (the Prayers slot). Grade logins' nav shown.
